# Missed-Call Rescue Templates

## Voicemail baseline

“Hi, you’ve reached {{business}}. We’re likely helping another customer right now, but we do call back quickly. Please leave your name, what you need help with, preferred timing, and the best number to text. For the fastest response, text this number with photos/details. Thanks.”

## Missed-call auto-text

“Thanks for calling {{business}} — sorry we missed you. What can we help with today? Send service needed, timing, location/neighborhood, and any photos/details. We’ll reply with next steps.”

## After-hours version

“Thanks for calling {{business}}. We’re closed for the day, but your message matters. Reply with what you need, ideal timing, and location. We’ll respond by {{next_business_day_time}}.”

## Callback opener

“Thanks for calling {{business}}; did I catch you at an okay time? I wanted to make sure we didn’t leave you hanging.”

## Lead tracker columns

Date, Name, Phone, Email, Service, Source, Urgency, Quote needed?, Next step, Follow-up date, Status, Notes

## Follow-up cadence

- T+0: auto-text immediately.
- T+15 minutes: callback if urgent/high-ticket.
- T+4 hours: “still need help?” text.
- T+24 hours: gentle final follow-up with opt-out.
